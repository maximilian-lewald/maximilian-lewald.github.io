Projection
On the sphere every keyword has neighbours in all directions — the plane has an edge, and whatever lands there gets pushed outward by it.
Idle spin
A slow drift when untouched — rotation is the strongest depth cue a sphere without a surface has.
Back side
How strongly the far side shows through. Drawn in flat grey so it reads as “behind” instead of competing with the front.
Source colours
Mixed blends the source colours by share — pale means evenly shared. Dominant shows the strongest source only.
Node repulsion
Higher = nodes push apart more, so clusters spread out and the structure becomes easier to see. Default 170. On release the map is laid out afresh from its starting point, so earlier settings leave no trace. The sphere needs no equivalent: its layout is final, because a repulsion with no edge to push against just spreads everything until the structure is gone.
Keywords
Upper limit for the number of keyword nodes. Keywords with fewer than 20 articles never make it onto the map, so the map can end up smaller.
Edges per keyword
Each keyword links to its N most similar keywords; edges are merged both ways, so most keywords end up with a few more. On the flat map the edges drive the layout — the more similar two keywords, the shorter and stronger the pull. The sphere only draws them, its layout is fixed.
Time spread
Drops keywords tied to a single event. The number is how much of the calendar spread a keyword reaches, as a share of what its article count allows — so a small source isn't punished for being small. “Bundestagswahl 2025” sits at 8 %, “Umwelt” at 85 %.
Ambiguity
Drops the keywords whose own neighbours agree with each other least — the ones sitting between two areas, which blur the structure without saying anything about a topic.
Similarity
How alike two keywords are: the cosine of their article vectors, with this share replaced by co-occurrence — the share of X's articles that also carry Y plus the share of Y's that carry X, halved. Co-occurrence pulls together what is reported together, across the language gap the cosine keeps. Shapes the edges and the repulsion between unlike keywords.
Keyword groups
Merged: keywords joined in the merge tool count as one node (“KI”, “AI”, “Künstliche Intelligenz”). Separate: each stands on its own, and the map shows where translations and spellings land without being joined.
